# Construct360

Construct360 is a workforce management web app made for ROCLA. It was built to help supervisors and admins manage their teams 
without any stress. It brings attendance tracking, leave approvals, and productivity monitoring together 
into one simple, easy to use system.

This app is made for the people running the business, not the workers on the ground. Employees have their own separate mobile 
app that they use to check in and view their own information. This web app is only used by admins and supervisors to manage and 
monitor their teams.

## Overview

Managing a large team can get messy. Keeping track of who came to work, who is late, who needs their leave approved, and how well 
the team is performing can take up a lot of time if it is all done manually. Construct360 was built to solve this problem.

With this app, a supervisor can log in, see everything about their team at a glance, and take action right away. No paperwork, no 
spreadsheets, no confusion.

## Who This App Is For

This app is built for supervisors and admins who are responsible for managing employees day to day. It is not meant to be used by 
employees. Employees interact with the system through a separate mobile app, which is designed just for them.

## Key Features

**Dashboard Overview**
As soon as a supervisor logs in, they land on a dashboard that gives them a full summary of their team. This includes how many people 
are on the team, how many are present today, how many leave requests are still pending, and how productive the team has been recently.

**QR Code Attendance Scanning**
Instead of writing down names or manually marking people present, supervisors can use their device's camera to scan each employee's 
personal QR code. As soon as it is scanned, that employee's attendance is recorded automatically.

**Attendance Tracking**
Every single check in and check out is recorded and organised into an easy to read attendance history. Supervisors can see who was 
present, who was late, and who was absent, along with the exact times they checked in and out.

**Productivity Monitoring**
This app also keeps track of how the team is performing over time. Supervisors can view daily, weekly, and overall productivity trends 
to understand how the team is doing compared to their targets.

**Leave Approval**
When an employee applies for leave through their mobile app, that request shows up here for the supervisor to review. From this page, 
a supervisor can approve or reject the request, and see the reason for the leave, how many days were requested, and the exact dates.

**Notifications**
Supervisors are kept in the loop through a notifications system. This alerts them whenever something needs their attention, such as 
a new leave request coming in or an employee missing work without notice.

## How It Works

1. The supervisor or admin opens the app and logs in using their email and password.
2. They are taken to their dashboard, where they can see a summary of everything happening with their team that day.
3. If they need to record attendance, they open the QR Scanner page and use their camera to scan each employee's ID card.
4. They can visit the Attendance page at any time to see a detailed history of everyone's check ins and check outs.
5. They can check the Productivity page to see how well the team is performing against their targets.
6. If any employee has requested leave, the supervisor goes to the Leave Approval page to either approve or reject it.
7. Throughout all of this, the Notifications page keeps them updated on anything important that needs their attention.

## User Roles

There are two types of users who can log into this web app.

**Admin**
Has full access to the system and can oversee everything happening across the business.

**Supervisor**
Manages their own team directly, including attendance, leave requests, and productivity tracking for the people under them.
